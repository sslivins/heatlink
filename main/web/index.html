Room sensors

Use wireless sensors on the wall as this room's temperature.
Battery-powered sensors on the wall report the real room temperature, over Bluetooth (BTHome, e.g. Shelly BLU H&T) or Wi-Fi (Shelly H&T Gen3). With up to 4 sensors the head uses their average. The unit heats or cools to that reading instead of the air at the head, and Off at setpoint no longer needs fan air checks. If every sensor goes quiet the head goes back to its own sensor.

Off at setpoint

Turns the head off once the room reaches its target so the fan stops, and back on when the room drifts past it. Heat, cool and dry only. The unit beeps each time it switches.
With the fan stopped the head only senses the air around itself. A short, quiet fan run moves room air past the sensor for a true reading.
Shared by every head on this outdoor unit (except the turn-back-on threshold).
--
Kept on while the zone group runs in Tandem.

Tandem™

An outdoor unit can heat or cool, but not both, so a warm room and a cold room can't both get comfortable. Tandem™ fixes that by taking turns heating and cooling, automatically. Rooms waiting for their turn are parked (switched off, settings kept) and restart on their own. Auto mode isn't available with Tandem.
--
Pair this head with the other heads on its outdoor unit in the Zones tab to use Tandem.
